BABY SHOP TILL — STEP 1: GET THE BLANK APP ONTO THE iPAD

You have 8 files. All of them go in the same place, together.
Do not rename any of them.

  index.html
  styles.css
  app.js
  service-worker.js
  manifest.webmanifest
  icon-180.png
  icon-192.png
  icon-512.png


PART A — PUT THE FILES ONLINE (once, needs internet)

1. Go to github.com and create a free account if you don't have one.

2. Click the + in the top right, then "New repository".
   Name it:  till
   Choose Public. Leave every other box alone. Click "Create repository".

3. On the next page click "uploading an existing file".
   Select all 8 files and upload them. Click "Commit changes".

4. Click "Settings" at the top, then "Pages" in the left list.
   Under "Branch" choose "main", leave the folder as "/ (root)", click Save.

5. Wait about two minutes, then refresh that page. It will show an address like:
   https://yourname.github.io/till/
   Write it down. That address is the app.


PART B — INSTALL IT ON THE iPAD (once, needs internet)

6. On the iPad, open Safari and go to that address.
   You should see "Baby shop till" and three checks.

7. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
   Scroll down, tap "Add to Home Screen", tap "Add".

8. Close Safari. Open the new icon on the Home Screen.

9. Close the app and open it again from the icon. This second open is what
   saves the offline copy.


PART C — THE TEST (this is the part that matters)

10. Turn on airplane mode.
11. Close the app completely: swipe up from the bottom and flick it away.
12. Open it again from the Home Screen icon.

It should open normally, and show:

   Opened from Home Screen ....... green
   Offline copy saved ............ green
   Internet ...................... grey, saying the app has no internet

If all three say that, step 1 is done. Tell me and we move to step 2.
If anything is different, tell me exactly what the screen says.
