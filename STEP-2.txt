STEP 2 — THE DATA TABLES

Three files to upload this time:

    index.html          (replaces the one already there)
    db.js               (new)
    service-worker.js   (replaces the one already there)

The two icon files and the manifest stay as they are. Don't touch them.


PART A — UPLOAD

1. On github.com, open your "till" repository.

2. Click "Add file", then "Upload files".

3. Drag in all three files. GitHub will replace the two that already
   exist and add db.js. That is what you want.

4. Scroll down, click "Commit changes".

5. Wait two minutes for GitHub to publish.


PART B — UPDATE THE iPAD

The iPad is holding its own offline copy, so it needs internet once to
pick up the new version.

6. Make sure Airplane Mode is OFF and the iPad has internet.

7. Open the till from the Home Screen icon.

8. Close it completely (swipe up from the bottom and hold, flick the
   card away), then open it again.

You should now see a list headed "Saved on this iPad" with four lines:
Products, Batches of stock, Sales, Items sold. All showing 0.

If you still see the old screen with "Working offline", close and
reopen once more. It sometimes takes two opens.


PART C — THE TEST

9. Tap "Save a test sale".

The numbers should change to: 1 product, 2 batches, 1 sale, 2 items
sold. Underneath, a panel appears showing what was sold.

Read that panel. It should say:

    2 × Test formula 800g at 185,000 ₭ = 370,000 ₭
    from batch LOT-A, expires 2026-12-31
    1 × Test formula 800g at 185,000 ₭ = 185,000 ₭
    from batch LOT-B, expires 2027-06-30
    Total 555,000 ₭
    Cash 600,000 ₭, change 45,000 ₭

That is the important bit. Three units were sold, and the till took two
from LOT-A before touching LOT-B — because LOT-A expires first. That is
first-expired-first-out working. It is the thing this whole batch system
exists for.

10. Now close the app completely and open it again.

The numbers must still be there. If they are, the till can remember
sales, and the foundation is finished.

11. Optional but worth doing: turn on Airplane Mode and tap "Save a test
    sale" again. It should save exactly the same way with no internet.
    The sales count goes to 2.

12. When you're happy, tap "Delete all test data" to clear the fake
    numbers out. Say yes to the warning.


WHAT THE TEST DATA IS

It is fake. One made-up product called "Test formula 800g" with a
made-up barcode and made-up prices. It exists only to prove the tables
work. The button that creates it gets removed when the real selling
screen is built.
